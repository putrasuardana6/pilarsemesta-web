Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Why choose an integrated Design & Build firm like Pilar Semesta over hiring separately? ↓

When architects and contractors operate separately, designs often exceed real construction budgets or prove impractical to build on-site, causing costly change orders. Pilar Semesta unifies architectural creativity, engineering accuracy, and fixed-price cost control under one roof.

How do you handle building permits (PBG/SLF) in Bali? ↓

We prepare the complete working drawing set—including architectural, structural calculation, and MEP engineering drawings—compliant with local zoning (ITR) and Indonesian building codes to ensure a smooth PBG (Persetujuan Bangunan Gedung) approval.

How can remote or overseas investors monitor site progress? ↓

We provide structured weekly progress reports via digital project tracking, including high-resolution job-site photography, quality control checklists, and milestone timeline tracking.
